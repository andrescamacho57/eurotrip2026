Our Trip
Greece & Portugal

July 31 — August 15, 2026

Two weeks. Islands, warm water, family, and a wedding. Everything is planned slowly, with lots of time to rest.

🇬🇷Athens2 nights
🏝️Paros5 nights
🇵🇹Lisbon4 nights
💒Ericeira3 nights

Day by day

One thing a day. Sometimes nothing at all.

✈️

Friday, July 31

We fly to Greece

Jacksonville, then Atlanta, then a night flight across the ocean.

Easy day. Wheelchair through both airports, so no long walks. Sleep on the plane.
🌿

Saturday, August 1

We land in Athens

We arrive in the morning and go straight to the hotel to rest.

Later, if we feel like it, a slow walk in the garden next door.

Dinner on the roof. You can see the Acropolis lit up from the table.
🏛️

Sunday, August 2

The Acropolis

A taxi takes us right to the gate. A little shuttle carries us up the hill. Then an elevator goes to the top.

No stairs, no climbing. Up there it is flat, and we walk together to the Parthenon and take our time.

After, the museum — cool and quiet inside.

Afternoon is for resting in the air conditioning. We can go in the morning or at sunset, whichever feels better that day.
🛫

Monday, August 3

We fly to Paros

A short flight, only 45 minutes. Then our hotel, right on the beach.

In the evening, a walk around the little fishing port and dinner by the water.

🏖️

Tuesday, August 4

Beach day

Nothing planned at all. Pool, beach, a long breakfast.

This is a do-nothing day. That is the whole plan.
🚗

Wednesday, August 5

Driving around the island

We have a car for the day and stop wherever we want.

A fishing village for coffee. A mountain town for lunch in the shade. A calm beach to swim. One more beach for sunset.

Short drives between each stop. Every place has somewhere to sit and a bathroom close by.
⛵️

Thursday, August 6

The boat day

A big wooden boat, all day on the water.

We see blue lagoons, sea caves, and a small island with an ancient temple on it.

Four swimming stops. We go in together with your life jacket, and Andres stays right beside you.

Lunch on the boat, fresh fruit, and drinks.

They saved you a seat. A couch in the middle of the boat with a good back, right next to the bathroom. Andres already asked them.
☀️

Friday, August 7

Rest day

The hotel, the beach, the little town. Maybe a sunset boat ride if you feel like it.

No pressure at all. We decide in the morning.
✈️

Saturday, August 8

We fly to Portugal

A long travel day, so we take it slowly. Wheelchair in the airports.

We have a long stop in Athens with time for a real sit-down dinner.

We sleep in the next morning. No alarm.
🥐

Sunday, August 9

Belém, by the river

Our hotel sits right on the water. Everything is flat and close by.

An old monastery, a modern museum, and the famous custard tarts — the real ones, from the bakery that invented them.

Dinner with family in the evening.

🛥️

Monday, August 10

Boat day with the family

Everyone comes. The boat leaves from the dock right outside our hotel, so there is no traveling to get there.

We go past the old tower, under the big red bridge, and out toward the statue on the hill.

Calm river water and hopefully a sunset.
🌊

Tuesday, August 11

An easy day in Lisbon

The pool, the spa, the river. Nothing we have to do.

Saving our energy for the wedding.
🏡

Wednesday, August 12

To Ericeira — the wedding begins

We drive out to the coast and move into a big house with the family. Five bedrooms, everyone together.

Dario's wedding starts.

💒

Thursday, August 13

The wedding

Long seafood lunches, sunsets over the ocean, and an afternoon at the family beach.

We rest in the room every afternoon. Wedding days are long and loud, so we take breaks on purpose.
🏊

Friday, August 14

Our last night

We move to a hotel on the cliffs with pools looking out over the ocean.

An easy, quiet night before the flight home.
🏠

Saturday, August 15

Home

Lisbon, then Paris, then Orlando.

Mom picks us up in Orlando and drives us home to Jacksonville.

Every single day

The same, no matter where we are

  • We never walk farWe go a little way, then we sit down. Taxis for anything longer.
  • A bathroom is always closeEvery place we go was chosen with that in mind.
  • The middle of the day is for restingAugust is hot. We go out early or in the evening, and rest with the air conditioning on in between.
  • Nothing bumpyNo fast boats, no rough rides. Calm water only.
  • Andres is never far awayNot once, the whole trip.

For the family

Every day on this trip has one thing on it at most, and several days have nothing at all. That is on purpose, not by accident.

If Mari needs to sit, stop, skip something, or go back to the room — that is the plan working, not the plan failing. Please don't wait to be asked.

The best things we do together are the slow ones: long lunches, the water, sunsets, sitting somewhere with a view. There is no schedule to keep up with.

Two weeks. Warm water, good food,
and no rushing anywhere.

See you in Greece ☀️
