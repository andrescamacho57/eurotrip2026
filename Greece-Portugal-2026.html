The Acropolis

  • Plan A — 8:00 AM. Coolest, quietest, least crowded.
  • Plan B — 5–7 PM. Site open to 8 PM, last entry ~7:30. Golden light, fading heat.
  • Taxi to the Green Gate (north side, Theorias St) → free shuttle vehicle → free elevator to the plateau
  • Restrooms at the entrance area — go before the lift
  • Other half of the day: Acropolis Museum — accessible, cool, quiet + long lunch
  • Midday: hotel AC rest. Non-negotiable in August.
Care: never walk from the hotel — it's 0.9 mi uphill. Chair covers the approach; she spends her steps on the flat plateau at the Parthenon. Free entry for her + you: bring passport and disability documentation. Budget ~½–¾ mi in small segments.

Standing rules

Non-negotiable

How this trip works

Read before improvising

  1. No jolts. No buggies, ATVs, speedboats, or bumpy rides. Cervical hardware can't absorb slamming. Catamarans, wooden boats, cars, calm water only.
  2. Heat. Big things before 10 AM or after 5 PM. Midday is AC rest, every day, no exceptions in August.
  3. Steps. Quarter-mile segments with breaks. Three-quarters is the hard ceiling. One to two miles cumulative. Chair on Jul 31, Aug 8, Aug 15.
  4. Restrooms. Access every one to two hours — six to eight stops a day is normal. Boats must have a toilet. Go-bag always packed: spare clothes, wipes.
  5. Boats. Morning departures, seating away from the bow, brief the crew about her neck. Swims are optional. Reschedule freely for wind.
  6. Sensory and seizure care. Pace loud crowded settings. Meds on schedule across time zones — Greece +7 from Florida, Portugal +5. Moderate alcohol around her.
  7. Never far apart. Every choice here keeps you within minutes of her: deliveries over counters, pickups over queues, private over logistics-heavy.
