Part 1 · Italian Alps
Alta Via 1 — Dolomites
June 18 (Toblach check-in) – June 25 (finish at Pissa) · 7-Day Trek
Alta Via 1 is one of the great long-distance hikes in Europe. You walk hut-to-hut across the Dolomites — one of the most dramatic mountain ranges on earth, a UNESCO World Heritage site. No resupply needed between days; each rifugio (mountain hut) provides dinner, bed, and breakfast. Bring cash for huts — cards often not accepted. Total distance: ~120 km, ~18,000 ft cumulative elevation gain over 7 days.

🍽 CROATIAN FOOD TO TRY

Days at Sea — Island Hopping
Hvar · Korčula · Vis · Mljet · Brač · Šolta
SailingActive RestLocal Food
▾
The Dalmatian Islands
Hvar
Hvar Town — the most glamorous island on the Croatian coast. Lavender fields, Renaissance architecture, cliff bars. Walk up to the fortress (Fortica) for panoramic views. Nightlife here is legendary if that's your thing.
Vis
Vis Island — remote, protected, off the tourist trail. The Blue Cave (Modra Špilja) on Biševo island is one of the natural wonders of the Mediterranean — water taxi required (~€15). Book through the skipper.
Korčula
Korčula Town — a perfectly preserved medieval walled city on a small peninsula. Often called "Little Dubrovnik." Walk the walls and eat at a konoba (local tavern) in the old town.
Mljet
Mljet National Park — lush green island with two saltwater lakes in the interior. Rent bikes or kayaks. Swim in the lakes. One of the most peaceful spots on the whole coast.
Brač
Zlatni Rat beach — Croatia's most famous beach, a golden pebble spit that changes shape with the current. Worth the stop.
🏃 FITNESS — Sail Week Routine
Each morning before the group wakes: swim 20–30 min off the back of the boat. When anchored near beaches, run or walk the coastline. Kayaking or paddleboarding when available counts as upper body. You won't maintain full mileage but you'll stay active and give your body the recovery it earned from the Dolomites.
🥩 NUTRITION ON BOARD
Half board = breakfast + dinner covered. Lunch is on you — usually at a harbour restaurant. Prioritize grilled fish at lunch (sea bass or bream, ~40g protein, low fat). Avoid fried calamari or burek (pastry) as your main. Protein powder in the morning if breakfast is light. Croatian fish is excellent and cheap on the islands — this is the easiest place to hit protein targets on the whole trip.
💡 Money: Croatia uses the Euro (€) since 2023. Card is accepted in towns; bring cash for water taxis and small konobas. Seasickness: If prone, take Dramamine the first night — open water can be rough. Water taxis: ~€50 total budgeted for side trips to caves, beaches, and transfers.

Part 3 · Finale
Dubrovnik
July 4 – July 8 · Fly home DBV 1:25 PM
Dubrovnik is the finale — one of the best-preserved medieval cities in the world, perched on a limestone peninsula above the Adriatic. 4 days to explore, recover, and properly savour the end of the trip. The City Walls walk is the centrepiece. Game of Thrones fans: most of King's Landing was filmed here.

City Walls Walk + Old Town
The must-do Dubrovnik experience
Culture ⭐Moderate
▾
Schedule
7:30A
City Walls — go early. The walls open at 8AM and get extremely crowded by 10AM. At dawn they're quiet and the light is perfect. 2km circuit around the entire old city above the Adriatic. About 1.5 hrs. Entrance ~€35.
10:00A
Breakfast on the Stradun. Croatian coffee culture is strong — sit and people watch.
11:00A
Explore the Old Town on foot — Rector's Palace, Dominican Monastery, Sponza Palace. Wander the side streets off the Stradun — that's where it gets real.
2:00P
Buža Bar — a bar cut into the cliff outside the city walls, literally hovering above the sea. Drinks and jumping off rocks into the Adriatic. One of the great spots in Croatia.
Evening
Dinner: Proto Restaurant or any fresh seafood konoba near the fish market. Order whatever fish was caught today.
🏃 FITNESS
City Walls = 2km + significant stair climbing — surprisingly physical. After Sail Week recovery, this is a good gentle re-activation. If you want a run: the Lapad Peninsula coastal path is the best running route in Dubrovnik — flat, along the sea, 3–5 miles.
🍽 FOOD TO TRY
Fresh Adriatic fish — whatever is on the daily board (orada, brancin, skuša). Prstaci — date mussels, a Dubrovnik delicacy. Rozata — Dubrovnik's version of crème caramel, the local dessert you can only really get here.
